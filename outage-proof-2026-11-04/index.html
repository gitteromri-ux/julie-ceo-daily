Longevity checkout outage: all independent evidence

Built Wed 4 Nov 2026, 20:50 Israel time (UTC+2). All times are Israel time unless marked UTC. Read-only collection; nothing was changed. No tokens or secrets on this page.

Bottom line

1. VERIFIED What the gate was. It was not an Access app on eteacher-leads-proxy.gitter-omri.workers.dev. Cloudflare Access is not even enabled on Omri's account (API error: "Access is not enabled"). The login page came from eTeacher's own Access team (eteachergroup.cloudflareaccess.com, "Azure AD ・ eTeacher Connect"), which sits in front of the eTeacher CRM API the Worker calls. The Worker passes a service token (secrets CRM_CF_ACCESS_CLIENT_ID/SECRET). When eTeacher's side stopped accepting it, the Worker returned eTeacher's login page with HTTP 200 instead of an order, and checkout stopped at "We could not confirm your enrollment request".

2. VERIFIED End: gate fixed 15:54 on 4 Nov. At 15:54:09 and 15:54:43 Omri (gitter.omri@gmail.com, from IP 213.57.115.180, dashboard) updated CRM_CF_ACCESS_CLIENT_ID and CRM_CF_ACCESS_CLIENT_SECRET. The first CRM order after that was at 15:59. The first full QA checkout that reached the payment form was at 16:08:54. "16:09" is the first verified success, not the moment of the fix. The true end is between 15:54:43 and 15:59.

3. NOT PROVEN Start at 21:00 on 3 Nov is NOT proven, and is contradicted. Blueprint e-commerce orders were still reaching the CRM until 22:07 on 3 Nov (orders 8070318–8070334, several of them Omri's own tests). So the gate started after 22:07 on 3 Nov VERIFIED. The earliest saved capture of the login page is 14:15 on 4 Nov VERIFIED. Bounds: start between 3 Nov 22:07 and 4 Nov 14:15. The narrowest estimate is from 22:07 to about 23:00 on 3 Nov, because Worker traffic stops after 22:00 and the last Purchase pixel was at 21:00 ESTIMATE. Neither our Cloudflare account nor any file on the box records when eTeacher changed or revoked the token. Only eTeacher's Access audit log can prove the start.

4. VERIFIED Not everything was down. Deals tagged "ecommerce ." with no product still reached the CRM at 00:03, 03:30–03:55 and 06:56 on 4 Nov. These probably came through the old /leads path, which goes to AWS and does not pass the Access gate ESTIMATE. The Blueprint and Masterclass checkout path (/api/lead/ecomm) created no orders from 22:37 on 3 Nov until 15:59 on 4 Nov. There was one exception: the Masterclass deal 8071107 at 10:01, tagged "Sync Course" rather than ecommerce, so it came from another source.

5. Real (non-test) orders since 16:09 today VERIFIED: Blueprint 8071646 at 19:03 (open, $0, not yet paid); Masterclass 8071365 at 17:18 (J. S., open, $0). Other "ecommerce ." deals at 17:22 (8071375) had no product. None of them is paid in ActiveCampaign as of 20:45.
