Terms in plain words

  • Spend: Money Meta charged.
  • Daily budget: Max Meta aims to spend per day.
  • Pacing: Share of the daily budget spent compared with share of the day already gone (Israel time). Ahead = spending fast.
  • Impressions: Times an ad was shown.
  • Reach: Different people who saw an ad.
  • Frequency: Average times each person saw it.
  • CPM: Cost per 1,000 impressions – price of attention. High CPM often = small or competitive audience.
  • Link click: A click that leaves Facebook/Instagram toward the site.
  • Link CTR: Link clicks ÷ impressions.
  • Landing page view (LPV): Click where the page actually started loading (Meta measured).
  • Cost per LPV: Spend ÷ landing page views.
  • View content / Add to cart / Initiate checkout / Purchase: Events the Meta pixel on the website reports.
  • Revenue (Meta purchase value): Order value the pixel reported, attributed to ads by Meta – not bank revenue.
  • MER / ROAS: Revenue ÷ spend. 1.0 = break-even on ad cost.
  • CPA: Cost per purchase = spend ÷ purchases.
  • VERIFIED / ESTIMATE: VERIFIED = read directly from Meta. ESTIMATE = my calculation or interpretation.
