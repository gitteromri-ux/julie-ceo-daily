What the terms mean

Spend
Money Meta charged today.
Daily budget
Max Meta aims to spend per day. "Campaign, shared" = budget set on the campaign and split across its ad sets.
Impressions
Times the ads were shown.
Reach
Different people who saw an ad.
Frequency
Average times each person saw an ad (impressions ÷ reach).
CPM
Cost per 1,000 impressions.
Link clicks
Clicks on the ad that go to the website.
Link CTR
% of impressions that got a link click.
Cost per link click
Spend ÷ link clicks.
Landing page views
Clicks where the website page actually loaded.
View content
Product/page view events fired by the pixel.
Add to cart
People who added the product to the cart (pixel event).
Initiate checkout
People who started the checkout (pixel event).
Purchases / value
Completed orders Meta credits to the ad set, and their value.
VERIFIED / CRM / ESTIMATE
VERIFIED = pulled live from Meta. CRM = paid orders in ActiveCampaign. ESTIMATE = calculated guess (none used here).
