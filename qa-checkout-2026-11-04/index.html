Longevity checkout and tracking audit

Run 4 Nov 2026, 14:10-14:40 Israel time. Read-only. No payment submitted, no card used, no setting changed.

Verdict: checkout is BROKEN on every device tested (VERIFIED).
  • Every buyer from the live ads lands on longevitylifeacademy.pages.dev.
  • At step 2 ("Continue" to Pay) the site sends the order to the lead service.
  • That service answers with an eTeacher Group company login page (Cloudflare Access, eteachergroup.cloudflareaccess.com, Microsoft sign-in) instead of a confirmation.
  • The site then shows "We could not confirm your enrollment request" and never opens the payment form.
  • Same failure on longevitylifeacademy.com: checkout page says "No enrollment in progress" and the card/PayPal form never loads.
  • So nobody can pay right now. That is also why no AddToCart, InitiateCheckout or Purchase has reached Meta since 3 Nov 21:00 Israel time.

  2. CRITICAL - No AddToCart / InitiateCheckout / Purchase can fire while #1 is open (they fire after the lead step). Meta last received AddToCart, InitiateCheckout and Purchase in the 3 Nov 21:00 hour (Israel time). VERIFIED from pixel stats.

  5. MEDIUM - AddPaymentInfo never fires (0 in 7 days, browser and server). VERIFIED.
  6. MEDIUM - .com homepage PageView fires from the browser only; no server (CAPI) PageView seen on .com. pages.dev sends both. VERIFIED on emulation.
  7. LOW - Meta diagnostics: "Pixel has low event source match rate" and "Pixel Missing Parameter in DPA Events" (catalog content_ids). VERIFIED from Graph API.
